What this application is

A private business portal used by First Front Solar Energy staff and its invited EPC clients to manage solar design projects. Accounts are created only by First Front Solar Energy; there is no public sign-up.

Information we process

  • Account details: name, login ID, role, email and phone number provided by the business.
  • Business records: leads, projects, quotations, purchase orders, invoices, attendance and leave.
  • Project files uploaded by staff or clients, and a log of who viewed or downloaded each file.

Google Drive

The portal stores project files in a Google Drive belonging to First Front Solar Energy, using Google's drive.file permission. This permission lets the application see and manage only the files and folders the application itself creates; it cannot read any other content in that Drive. Google user data is used solely to store and retrieve the business's project files, is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is kept and who can see it

Records are stored with Supabase (database hosting) and files in Google Drive. Access is limited by role: clients see only their own projects and the files released to them; staff see what their role requires.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as First Front Solar Energy needs it for its business and legal obligations. To ask for access to or deletion of your personal data, contact the email address above. Access to Google Drive can be withdrawn at any time from your Google Account's security settings.
